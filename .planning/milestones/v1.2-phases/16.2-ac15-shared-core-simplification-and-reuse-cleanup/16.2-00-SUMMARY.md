## Decisions Made

- EF is the only repository/unit-of-work abstraction for these AC15 flows; the correction does not replace rejected interfaces with renamed equivalents.
- Era branching is kept inside shared services where table/entity ownership differs, while Green AI/ghost handling remains a hook because it has real era-specific behavior.
- Mapperly is used for straightforward record/entity projection; merge policy, unlock policy, timestamps, EF keys, and special-mode gates remain explicit service code.
