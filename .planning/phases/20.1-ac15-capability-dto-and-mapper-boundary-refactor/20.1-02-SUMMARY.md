# Phase 20.1 Plan 02: Blue/Green BAID Controller Assembly Summary

**Blue and Green BAID responses now use controller-owned final assembly with Mapperly-generated section application only**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 24 min
- **Started:** 2026-06-14T08:18:00Z
- **Completed:** 2026-06-14T08:42:12Z
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 9

## Accomplishments

- Replaced Blue/Green `BaidResponseMapper.Map(Ac15BaidResponse)` aggregate creation with controller-instantiated `BAIDResponse` objects.
- Converted Blue/Green BAID mappers to source-strict existing-target `Apply(section, response)` declarations for identity, profile, customization inventory, shop medals, Dan status, and compatibility.
- Moved `ContentInfo`, route constants, BAID, access code, publish/player flags, and Blue compatibility defaults into controllers.
- Removed Blue/Green handler population of temporary alias properties now that their controllers consume semantic sections.
- Updated Blue/Green tests away from aggregate BAID mapper calls and adjusted the Yellow compatibility shape test to assemble its Blue comparison through the new Blue controller-style path.
