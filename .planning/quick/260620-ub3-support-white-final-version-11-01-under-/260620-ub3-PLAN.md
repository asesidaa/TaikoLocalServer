# Quick Task 260620-ub3: White Final 11.01 `/v07r03` Support

## Goal

Support White final version 11.01 under `/v07r03/chassis/*` using the dumped `proto/white-final` game protocol while preserving existing White handler behavior and adding only the final-version surfaces called out by the user: Tokkun mode and the difficulty selection panel fields.

## Source Facts

- `proto/white-final/taiko.proto` is the protocol input. Do not edit dumped proto files.
- Final White keeps the existing direct-protobuf White transport and adds final fields including `difficulty_tutorial_flg`, `difficulty_played_course`, `difficulty_played_star`, `tokkun_tutorial_flg`, and `ary_tokkunstage_info`.
- Current White routes are `/v07r00/chassis/*`; final White must be reachable at `/v07r03/chassis/*`. Preserve `/v07r00` as a compatibility alias unless implementation evidence proves it conflicts with final White.
- Current White playresult mapping intentionally maps Tokkun to `null` and ignores difficulty panel fields. This quick task supersedes that for final White.
- Current White save state already has difficulty tutorial/course/star fields, but no nullable White Tokkun tutorial field and no White Tokkun stage-history table.
- Do not add a new adapter project. Reuse the existing `Adapters.GameProtocol.White` adapter, Application AC15 helpers, White-owned EF state, and Mapperly source-generated mapper pattern.
- Do not invent unrelated final White behavior: no Banacoin wallet/payment/coupon authority, no gacha state, no ChallengeCompe cabinet route/readback, no battle state, and no broad AdminApi/WebUI additions.
